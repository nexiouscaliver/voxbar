## A Honesty Pass Over The Whole App

Two full review cycles (a 36-reviewer fleet across every feature and toggle,
with a shared knowledge base between rounds) turned up a pattern: things that
silently failed. This release closes roughly fifty of those findings.

## Notices That Actually Reach You

When the recording card cannot show a message (the overlay is off, or you are
between sessions), important notices now reach you anyway: a toast when the
window is open, and a real macOS notification when it is not. The companion
session limit, command-mode with no session, update prompts, and every
card-less notice now land somewhere you can see. Picking Post-process Prompt
options in the menu bar while the feature is off no longer pretends to work;
the menu greys out.

## Hotkeys That Do Not Die Silently

Rebinding a shortcut onto a chord another action owns is now rejected up front
with a message naming the owner, instead of leaving one of the two dead.
Turning a feature on when its key cannot register tells you and rolls the
toggle back. Switching keyboard implementations mid-recording keeps the cancel
key armed. Closing Settings while recording a shortcut no longer suspends
every hotkey. Shortcut rows grey out while their feature toggle is off.

## Toggles That Tell The Truth

Autostart failures now surface and roll the toggle back. Turning Experimental
off actually stops the companion server. A failed microphone switch restores
your previous microphone instead of pointing settings at one that cannot open.
History retry no longer sends stored transcripts to a cloud provider when
post-processing is off. Re-enabling update checks no longer fires a silent
network check at toggle time, and the app language only switches after the
choice is actually saved.

## Smaller Repairs

Onboarding survives an offline model catalog with Retry and Skip. Settings
search now covers App Language, Update Policy, Theme, and Accent Color.
Failed model loads, accelerator probes, and update relaunches report honestly.
In-app release notes now cover 1.3.0 and 1.4.0 as well.
