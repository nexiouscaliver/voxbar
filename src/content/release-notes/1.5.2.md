## Update Honesty

An automatic update whose installation failed used to show a card claiming it
had been installed, stacked on top of the error message, with two system
notifications for one event. The success card now appears only after a
successful install.

## Settings That Match Reality

A failed settings change rolled back a whole stale snapshot, quietly undoing
unrelated changes made in the same moment; it now restores only the key that
failed. The menu bar keeps its prompt list, post-process provider, model list,
and unload-after checkmark current after every change made in Settings, and
history limits clean up old entries before saving so the saved value always
matches what the cleanup could actually do.

## Stability

A failed input-channel switch brings the microphone stream back instead of
leaving it cold, and a second phone connecting mid-dictation can no longer
steal the session badge or take the blame in session notices.
