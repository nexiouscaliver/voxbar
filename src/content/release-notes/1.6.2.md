## Fixed: Tap-Locked Phone Sessions Capture Your Speech

Tap to lock had one more flaw hiding under the double-tap fix from the
last release: the session stayed locked and live, but the Mac closed its
audio admission window the moment your finger lifted from the tap, so a
quick tap captured only the sliver of the press itself and everything you
said after it was dropped as out-of-session audio, ending with the
nothing-captured notice. Admission now follows the live session state of
the phone's own lane, checked at every received audio frame, so a locked
session keeps capturing speech until you tap again to stop it. The
fifteen-minute safety cap still force-finalizes a locked session left
running, and the idle button and welcome hint on the phone now name both
modes: hold to talk, tap to lock.

## New: True Multi-Phone Dictation

Every paired phone now gets its own recording session. Two people can
dictate at the same time from two phones, and one phone's stop or Escape
never ends, mutes, or discards the other's session. Each phone's history
list and "recent dictations" are its own, and the recording card names
the phone that is talking.

Cancel is finally honest on the phone: pressing Escape for a phone session
now tells that phone to stop streaming and un-sticks its button immediately.
Before this fix the phone kept sending audio that was silently dropped, its
button stayed in the recording state, and the session stayed live until the
app was restarted.

Phone dictations also start their transcription right away now. A fixed
two-second wait that every phone session paid between the stop tap and the
transcription starting is gone.

## Fixed: Your History Database Is Treated More Carefully

When the history database cannot be opened at launch, the app moves the
file aside, starts a fresh database in its place, and tells you with one
persistent notification that names the quarantined file. The quarantine
fires only when the file itself is provably corrupt: a database written
by a newer VoxBar build, or one that was merely busy or hit a transient
disk error, stays in place and intact instead of being replaced with an
empty one.

## Fixed: History Retries Keep Their Settings

Retrying an entry from History now uses the provider, model, and prompt
the entry was recorded with, instead of silently dropping them and
re-running with whatever is currently selected. The summary the entry
shows is rewritten together with the new text, so the polished marker on
a retried entry always matches what really ran. Deletions update the
visible list immediately whether you delete by hand or the retention
cleanups do, and a deleted built-in prompt template stays deleted across
restarts.

## Fixed: Recording and Microphone Reliability

- Starting a Mac dictation in the same instant as a lid open or close no
  longer silently discards the recording: the microphone re-resolution
  that follows a lid change now waits for live recordings instead of
  restarting the capture underneath them.
- Flipping the laptop lid during on-demand mode's idle window no longer
  leaves the microphone hot indefinitely.
- The microphone chosen for a closed lid is applied right away, not only
  saved for the next launch.
- A custom words list containing a blank entry no longer breaks the
  dictation that uses it.
- With several phones recording, cancelling one session no longer wedges
  the tray and overlay states of the others.

## Fixed: Tailscale, Labeled Honestly

An interface in the Tailscale address range is only labeled Tailscale
when the interface name corroborates it. A carrier-grade NAT address from
your ISP that happens to share that range now labels and pairs as plain
LAN, and the pairing subnet follows the honest label.

## Fixed: A Round of Interface Polish

- With the overlay turned off, the paste lands immediately instead of
  waiting on a preview that was never going to show.
- A session that starts inside the error card's short hide delay no
  longer has its card torn down by the stale hide.
- The star on a history entry keeps its own saving state, restores the
  previous value when the save fails, and tells you about the failure.
- Buttons on accent fills flip their label color when you pick a
  non-default accent, and the AppData and Log Directory buttons open the
  folders themselves instead of revealing them in a parent.
- The settings page survives a rendering error in one pane instead of
  taking the whole app down.
- Custom start and stop cues fall back to the default theme's cue when
  the custom one is missing, the microphone permission Check Again
  button answers with the permission's current state, and the debug
  chord ignores held-key auto repeats.

## Fixed: A Harder Working Pipeline

- Prompt Studio batches gained a Cancel button that stops the run between
  evaluations, so a mis-picked batch no longer has to run to the end.
- The initial prompt handed to Whisper is bounded and your custom words
  ride the tail, so a long words list can no longer push the
  instructions out of the window.
- Apple Intelligence calls run on the dedicated blocking pool, so a long
  AI post-process no longer parks the runtime worker that everything
  else waits behind.
- Transcription and model errors arrive as readable localized messages
  instead of raw backend sentences, and terminal punctuation is script
  aware, with CJK fullwidth marks and Devanagari endings handled
  correctly.

## Fixed: Tray Copy Tells You What Happened

The tray's "Copy last transcript" actions now distinguish "there is nothing
to copy yet" from "the clipboard write failed", so the notification you get
names the actual problem, and the message also reaches the in-app
notification log when desktop notifications are turned off.

## Behind The Scenes

The checks that used to be run by hand now run in CI on every change:
translation parity across every locale, the text style rules, and the
model-language catalog. The version pin now fails any incomplete bump, so
a release cannot ship without its manifests, its fallback, and its release
notes all agreeing.
