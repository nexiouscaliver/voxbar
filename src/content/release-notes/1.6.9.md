# VoxBar 1.6.9

## The freeze after phone dictation is fixed at the root

If you use your phone as a dictation mic, you may have hit the freeze where the whole app stopped responding after a dictation and had to be force quit. We found the actual cause this time, with macOS's own hang reports and a thread-by-thread analysis of the wedged app.

**What was happening.** After every phone dictation, VoxBar refreshes the recent-history list on each connected phone. The loop that walked the connected phones held the internal connection registry locked while it was sending, and the send itself needed the same lock. That is a deadlock with itself: one thread waiting for a lock it was already holding. The freeze only appeared later, whenever something else needed that registry (quitting the app, the next phone connection, a tray refresh), which is why it felt random and could even surface hours after the dictation.

**The fix.** The loop now takes its snapshot of connected phones first and releases the lock before sending anything. A regression pin also forbids any loop in the companion code from holding a lock while its body runs, so this class of bug cannot quietly come back.

## A watchdog so a stall can never freeze VoxBar again

Bugs get fixed, but the rule going forward is: nothing inside VoxBar may ever leave it hanging. A small watchdog now checks the core internal state every few seconds, entirely off to the side. If something ever stops responding for about 15 seconds, VoxBar shows a notification saying it recovered from an internal stall and restarts itself cleanly. Dictation keeps working; you should never have to force quit. Quitting is protected too: if the shutdown path ever gets stuck, a five-second fuse completes the exit instead of leaving a zombie.

The watchdog is a setting (Advanced, Stability, on by default) and can be turned off if you prefer.
