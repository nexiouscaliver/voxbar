## Fixed: The Stuck Transcribing Overlay After a Phone Dictation

After a phone dictation finished and its text arrived on the phone, the
Mac could freeze its own teardown: the transcribing overlay stayed on
screen forever, and no new recording would start, from the phone or from
the keyboard, until the app was force quit.

The cause was one panic in the wrong place. The settings store rides a
plugin whose internal registration can go stale (it can end up naming a
resource id the app no longer holds). Once that happened, every settings
read failed, and the read inside the stop pipeline's teardown turned
that failure into a panic. The panic cut the teardown in half: the
overlay and the tray never retired, and the poisoned store locks took
every new session down with them, which is why nothing recorded
afterward.

The settings store acquisition now heals itself. When the plugin
registration is dead, it is rebuilt on the spot and the settings are
reloaded from disk, so nothing you configured is lost. If the store
still cannot be mounted, reads fall back to the shipped defaults and
writes skip that one save with a logged error instead of taking the app
down. And the entire stop pipeline is now panic-contained: any future
failure in its tail retires the overlay and the tray as a logged
one-session defect rather than a wedge that needs a force quit.

Both fixes carry machine-checked source pins, and the diagnosis was
reproduced end to end against the shipping app (thread stacks sampled
mid-wedge, then the panic captured under instrumentation) before the
fix was written.
