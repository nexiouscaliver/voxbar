## Fixed: Phone Dictation Audio Actually Transmits Now

The v1.6.2 phone client carried one defect that outranked every other:
the resampler that converts your phone's microphone audio for the Mac
corrupted a sample at every internal block boundary, and the Mac's
voice activity detector read the poisoned stream as pure silence. Your
speech left the phone, but the Mac recorded zero samples and answered
with the nothing-captured notice, in both hold and tap-to-lock modes,
every single time. The boundary sample is now carried across blocks
exactly, and every outgoing sample is checked, so a corrupted value
cannot reach the wire by construction. The Mac also scrubs any stray
corrupted sample from a misbehaving client to silence, counts what it
caught, and warns the first time it happens. What you say into the
phone arrives.

## Fixed: The Level Meter Stops Lying

The phone's level bar used to dance over audio whether or not any of it
was being sent, and it sampled only every eighth sample of the raw
capture. During the silence defect above it kept showing a lively bar
while the Mac received nothing at all. The bar now peaks over every
sample of the audio that actually passed the send gate, and when the
gate is closed it decays to empty and dims, so a lively meter always
means live transmission.

## New: Zero-Audio Advice That Names Your Mode

When a session ends with no audio at all, the recovery notice now
speaks in the mode you were using: hold the button and then speak, tap
the button and then speak, or hold or tap, worded for the way the
session was actually started. The advice ships in every supported
language, and the generic hint is gone.

## Behind The Scenes: Dropped Audio Is Counted Out Loud

Audio the Mac admits but has to drop because the phone's recording lane
is already closed is no longer eaten silently. Each phone's connection
counts its own dropped chunks, logs a warning the first time one lands,
and reports the total when the phone disconnects, so the log can say
what happened to a dictation instead of leaving it to a forensic pass.
